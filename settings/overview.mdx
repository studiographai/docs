Two places in the account menu (your avatar in the top-right corner) hold everything you can configure:

- **Settings** — your own preferences, the archive, and, for Owners, the workspace itself.
- **Studio** — connectors to outside tools, and MCP access for tools that connect to your workspace.

Your account, your team, billing, and your AI plan live in the **Studiograph Cloud** console. The account menu's **Account** item opens it in a new tab.

## Settings

| Section | What's there | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [**Preferences**](#preferences) | Appearance, sidebar order, default folder, exporting your data, and your profile | Everyone |
| [**Archive**](#archive) | Archived folders and recently deleted entries | Everyone |
| [**Workspace**](#workspace) | The workspace's name, description, and time zone | Owners |
| [**Team**](#team) | Who's in the workspace | Owners |
| [**Entries**](#entries) | The catalog of entry types | Owners |
| [**AI & services**](/settings/ai-plan) | Opens your AI plan in Studiograph Cloud | Owners |

Members don't see the owner-only sections at all.

### Preferences

- **Accent color** and **Appearance** (**Light**, **Dark**, or **System**).
- **Sidebar folders** — keep folders in the order you drag them into (**However I arrange them**), or **Alphabetically**.
- **Default folder** — where new entries, imported files, and saved meetings go when you haven't picked a folder.
- **Startup** — in the Studiograph desktop app only, whether Studiograph opens when you log in to your computer.
- **Your data** — **Export** downloads a zip of every folder you can access, with its history.
- **Member details** — your name and email, with a **Manage profile in Studiograph Cloud** link for changing them, and **Resume onboarding interview** if you paused it.

### Archive

Everything here can be brought back.

- **Folders** — folders that have been archived. Choose **Restore** to put one back in the sidebar with all of its entries. Folder admins and Owners can also choose **Delete permanently**, which asks you to type the folder's name to confirm and can't be undone.
- **Entries** — entries deleted in the last 30 days, newest first; **Show older** loads more of the list. Choose **Restore** to put one back where it was. You can restore an entry if you can edit the folder it was in.

See [Folders](/workspace/folders) for archiving and deleting folders.

### Workspace

The workspace's **Description** and **Time zone** — the zone schedules and dates are read in. The workspace's name is managed in Studiograph Cloud: renaming it there renames it here.

### Team

The workspace's **Members**, with each person's role. People are added, removed, and given roles in Studiograph Cloud — **Manage in Studiograph Cloud** takes you there, and changes sync to the workspace automatically. See [Your account](/getting-started/your-account#roles).

To give someone access to a folder, use that folder's **Share** dialog. See [Sharing](/workspace/sharing).

### Entries

The catalog of entry types your workspace uses — rename and restyle them, turn them on or off, or add your own. See [Entry types](/settings/entry-types).

### AI & services

Opens the **AI** page of the Studiograph Cloud console, where you choose whether Studiograph runs the AI for you or you bring your own keys. See [Your AI plan](/settings/ai-plan).

## Studio

| Section | What's there | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Connectors** | Outside tools the agent can use on your behalf | Everyone connects their own account; Owners add connectors |
| **MCP access** | Your workspace's MCP endpoint, the apps you've connected, and personal access tokens | Everyone |

See [Connectors](/agent/connectors) and [Connect AI tools](/ai-tools/mcp).

<Accordion title="On a self-hosted workspace">
A few teams run Studiograph on their own server instead of Studiograph Cloud. Their Settings differ in a few places:

- **Team** is where Owners add members (name, email, starting password, and role). Opening a member shows their **Role**, their **Folders** (with a per-folder Admin or Editor role), **Reset password**, and **Delete member**. Deleting a member moves their personal folder to you, renamed "Archive: …", and makes you admin of any shared folder where they were the only admin.
- **Workspace** also lets Owners rename the workspace.
- **Providers** replaces **AI & services**. It holds the model provider keys (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, or OpenRouter) with the **Default model** and **Image generation** model, plus **Asset storage**, **Web search**, and **Semantic search**.
- **Meeting capture** appears in the Studiograph desktop app, for choosing the transcription engine — **Apple Intelligence** on your Mac, or **Deepgram** with an API key.
- **Preferences → Member details** is where you change your own name, email, and password.
</Accordion>
