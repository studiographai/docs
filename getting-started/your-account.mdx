Studiograph runs on **Studiograph Cloud**: your team gets its own hosted workspace, and your account, your team, and your AI plan are managed at [studiograph.com](https://studiograph.com). Studiograph Cloud calls a workspace a **studio** — it's the same thing.

## Creating a workspace

Studiograph Cloud is in private alpha. Click **Request alpha access** on [studiograph.com](https://studiograph.com) to join the list. When your invitation arrives, its link takes you through creating an account and setting up your workspace in four steps:

1. **Create studio** — name your workspace.
2. **Activate access** — start your plan.
3. **Connect AI** — choose whether Studiograph runs the AI for you or you bring your own provider keys. See [Your AI plan](/settings/ai-plan).
4. **Open studio** — go to your new workspace.

The person who creates the workspace is its first **Owner**.

## Joining a team

You don't create your own account on an existing workspace — someone on the team invites you. An Owner or Admin opens the Studiograph Cloud console, goes to **Team**, and clicks **Invite**, entering your email address and a role. Accept the invitation from the email you receive, and you're in.

## Signing in

Go to your workspace's address (it looks like `https://<your-workspace>.studiograph.ai`). Studiograph sends you to Studiograph Cloud to sign in, then brings you back. You can also sign in at [studiograph.com](https://studiograph.com) and click **Open studio**.

You stay signed in for seven days. To sign out, open the account menu (your avatar in the top-right corner) and choose **Sign out**.

## Roles

Studiograph Cloud has three roles:

| Role | In Studiograph Cloud | In the workspace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Manages billing, the team, and the AI plan, and can make other people Owners | Everything a Member can do, plus the owner-only settings: **Workspace**, **Team**, **Entries**, and **AI & services** |
| **Admin** | Invites teammates and manages the AI plan | The same as a Member |
| **Member** | — | Full everyday use: create folders and entries, share folders you administer, and use the agent |

Owners change a teammate's role, or remove them, on the console's **Team** page. Changes reach the workspace automatically.

Access to individual folders is separate from your role: within a folder you're either an **Admin** or an **Editor**. See [Sharing](/workspace/sharing).

## Your profile

Your name and email belong to your Studiograph Cloud account. In the workspace, **Settings → Preferences** shows them under **Member details**, with a **Manage profile in Studiograph Cloud** link for changing them. The **Account** item in the account menu opens the Studiograph Cloud console in a new tab.

## The onboarding interview

The first time you sign in, the agent greets you in the chat panel with a short interview to get to know you and your work. Owners get a longer interview that also sets up the workspace, starting with your organization's website; members get five questions about themselves and their role.

Answer at your own pace. To pass on a question, type "skip" and the agent moves on. To stop for now, click the **×** on the **Onboarding Interview** pill above the message box and choose **Pause**. Resume any time from **Settings → Preferences** with **Resume onboarding interview**.

## Your starter folder

Every new member gets one private folder, named **{"{First name}"}'s Folder** (or with your full name, if a teammate shares your first name). It's an ordinary folder — rename it, move things into it, share it, or delete it if you don't want it. It stays private until you share it, and if you delete it, it isn't recreated. See [Folders](/workspace/folders).

## The Getting Started folder

A new Studiograph Cloud workspace comes with a **Getting Started** folder, shared with everyone. It holds a **Start here** entry plus templates, examples, and [skills](/agent/skills) for presentations, publications, diagrams, infographics, landing pages, social media, working with data, and your brand. Use it as a starting point, change it to suit your team, or archive it once you no longer need it.

<Accordion title="On a self-hosted workspace">
A few teams run Studiograph on their own server instead of Studiograph Cloud. There, accounts work differently:

- An Owner adds each member in **Settings → Team** with a name, email, starting password, and role. There is no invitation email.
- You sign in at the workspace's address with your email and password. After five failed attempts within 15 minutes, sign-in is locked until that window ends.
- A brand-new workspace shows a setup page — **Create your admin account to get started** — and whoever completes it becomes the Owner.
- There are two roles, Owner and Member. Owners change a member's role in **Settings → Team**.
- You change your own name, email, and password in **Settings → Preferences**.
</Accordion>
