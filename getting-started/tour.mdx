## The top bar

From left to right:

- **New** — creates entries and folders. See [Creating things](#creating-things) below.
- **Home**, **Recent**, and **Library** — the three places that aren't a folder. **Recent** lists entries from every folder you can access; the **Library** does the same for files. See [Library](/library/overview).
- **Search** — see [Search](#search) below.
- **Notifications** — the bell tells you when someone mentions you in a comment.
- **The account menu** — your avatar in the top-right corner. See [Settings and the Studio](#settings-and-the-studio).

## The sidebar

The left sidebar lists every folder you can access. Folders nest, and a glyph beside each one shows whether it's private, shared with specific people, or open to everyone. See [Folders](/workspace/folders).

Each folder's **…** menu holds **Share**, **New entry**, **New subfolder…**, and **Import files…**. Folder admins also see **Move to…** and **Settings…**, where a folder is renamed, archived, or deleted.

To choose how folders are ordered, open **Settings → Preferences → Sidebar folders**: **However I arrange them** lets you drag folders into place, and **Alphabetically** keeps them sorted.

## Home

Home opens on today's date with two lists:

- **Meetings** — meetings for **Today**, the **Next two days**, or **This week**. Click one to open its entry.
- **Tasks** — open tasks assigned to you for **Today**, **This week**, or everything **Upcoming**. Overdue tasks are grouped at the top and tasks with no date (**Any time**) at the bottom; within each group, tasks are sorted by priority.

Each list starts with a **New meeting** or **New task** row, so you can add one without leaving home. Check a task's box to complete it, or set its due date right from the row.

## Search

Search lives in the top bar. Press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux) from anywhere to start typing. Results come from every folder you can access, and chips at the top of the results narrow them to one entry type. Press Enter, or choose **See all results**, to open the full results page.

## Creating things

Click **New** in the top bar:

- **New note** creates a note right away.
- **New event** and **New task** open the new-entry dialog on the fields those entries need.
- **More types…** opens the full entry type picker. You can also press **N** anywhere.
- **New folder** creates a top-level folder. To nest one, use **New subfolder…** in a folder's **…** menu.

To bring in files, use **Import files…** in a folder's menu, the **Import** button in the Library, or drag files onto a folder. See [Importing files](/library/importing-files).

On a phone, a round **+** button in the lower right takes the place of **New**.

## The agent

The chat panel on the right is the agent — the built-in AI that can search, read, create, and edit across everything you have access to. Press **⌘/** (**Ctrl+/**) to open or close it, or **⌘J** (**Ctrl+J**) to open it on a new chat. See [Chatting with the agent](/agent/chat).

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** | Search |
| **N** | New entry |
| **⌘/** / **Ctrl+/** | Open or close the agent |
| **⌘J** / **Ctrl+J** | Open the agent on a new chat |
| **⌘,** / **Ctrl+,** | Hide or show the sidebar |
| **⌘.** / **Ctrl+.** | Hide or show both side panels |

## Settings and the Studio

Everything about your account and workspace is in the account menu (your avatar in the top-right corner):

- **Settings** — your **Preferences** and the **Archive** of archived folders and deleted entries. Owners also see the workspace settings. See [Settings](/settings/overview).
- **Studio** — **Connectors** to outside tools the agent can use, and **MCP access** for connecting tools like Claude and Codex to your workspace. See [Connectors](/agent/connectors) and [Connect AI tools](/ai-tools/mcp).
- **Account** — opens your Studiograph Cloud account in a new tab.
- **Theme** — **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**.
