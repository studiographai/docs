title: "Views"
description: "See your entries as a list, gallery, board, calendar, or graph — and shape them with filters and arrangement."
---

Every folder can be seen five different ways, and so can **Recent** and the full search results. Pick a view from the row of view icons in the toolbar, then shape it with **Filters** and **Arrange**.

The view you pick follows you: switch to Board in one folder, and the next folder you open is a board too. Your choice is remembered on this device, and each browser tab keeps its own — handy for a calendar in one tab and a list in another. Filters stay on as you move between folders in the same tab; the dot on **Filters** and the "hidden" count at the bottom of the screen tell you when some entries are filtered out.

## The five views

- **List** — a table of [entries](/workspace/entries) in this folder, with subfolders listed first. The columns come from the entry type. Sort by clicking a column header, or group rows by a field.
- **Gallery** — an image grid, best for visual work. It shows the entries that have an image; the others are counted as hidden. If nothing has an image, **View as list** switches back.
- **Board** — entries as cards in lanes, grouped by a field like status, stage, priority, category, or assignee. Drag a card to another lane to change that field; cards without a value gather in a leading **(Empty)** lane. You can also right-click a card and choose **Change lane**.
- **Calendar** — plots each entry on its main date (see below), in month or week layout. An entry with a start date spans the days from start to finish. Drag an entry to a different day to reschedule it, or click an empty spot to add a new event there. In week view, drag across the hours to add a timed event, or drag an event's bottom edge to change how long it lasts.
- **Graph** — entries as connected nodes, drawn from the links between them. See [Links, search & the graph](/workspace/connections#the-graph).

Board, Calendar, and Graph include entries from subfolders; List and Gallery show the folder's own entries, with its subfolders.

## Filters and arrangement

**Filters** narrows what's shown: by **Entry type**, by the type's own fields (status, client, and so on), by **Tags**, by when entries were **Created** or by their dates, and by **Created by**. People fields include a **Me** option. **Clear all** removes every filter.

On a board, the **Entry type** filter also decides which type's lanes you see — filter to one type for its full set of lanes.

**Arrange** sets the order:

- **Sort by** a field, **Ascending** or **Descending**.
- **Group by** a field, in List.
- **Lanes** — the field a Board groups by.

On Calendar, the same spot switches between month and week, and **‹ Today ›** moves between periods.

## Working with several entries

Tick the checkboxes on rows, tiles, or cards to select several entries. The selection bar offers **Edit fields…** (in List and Gallery), **Change lane** (in Board), **Move to…**, **Copy to…**, **Download**, and **Delete**.

## Which date the calendar uses

Each type has one main date, and Calendar plots it:

| Type | Date plotted |
| --- | --- |
| Task | Due date |
| Deliverable | Due date |
| Deal | Close date |
| Project | End date |
| Event | End date (spans from the start date) |

An Owner can put another type on the calendar — or take one off — with **Show on calendar** in [Settings → Entries](/settings/entry-types#show-a-type-on-the-calendar).
