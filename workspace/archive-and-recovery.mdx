Most things you remove in Studiograph can be brought back. This page covers what's recoverable, where to find it, and the few actions that are permanent.

## The Archive

Open the account menu (your avatar in the top-right corner), choose **Settings**, then **Archive**. Everyone can open it, and each person sees what they're allowed to restore.

### Archived folders

**Folders** lists folders that have been archived. Archiving deletes nothing — the folder and everything in it is simply hidden from the sidebar, search, and the library.

- Open a folder's **…** menu and choose **Restore** to put it back, with all of its entries.
- **Delete permanently** removes it for good, after you type its name to confirm.

Folder admins see the folders they administer; workspace Owners see every archived folder. To archive a folder in the first place, see [Folders](/workspace/folders#archiving-a-folder).

### Deleted entries

**Entries** lists entries deleted in the last 30 days, newest first; **Show older** loads more of the list. Choose **Restore** to put an entry back where it was — you can restore an entry if you can edit the folder it was in.

This includes the entries of a deleted subfolder. If an entry's folder has since been archived, restoring it offers to bring the folder back too.

The list is a window on your workspace's history, not a trash can: nothing needs emptying, and entries deleted more than 30 days ago simply aren't listed. For older deletions, ask the agent.

## Earlier versions of an entry

Every entry keeps its own history. Open the entry's **…** menu, choose **Panels → History**, and pick a version to preview it, see what changed, or **Restore** it. See [Version history](/workspace/collaboration#version-history).

## Asking the agent

The agent can do this for you: "bring back the brief I deleted last week" or "restore yesterday's version of this page". It looks through deletions from the last 90 days — further back than the Archive shows — and through an entry's history, and acts as you, so it can only restore what you could.

## What can't be undone

- **Deleting a top-level folder** removes the folder, its entries, and its files permanently. Anything already deleted from that folder can no longer be restored either. Archive a folder instead if you might need it later.
- **Deleting a file** from the library is permanent.
- **Files deleted along with an entry** — for example, the original of an imported document — don't come back when the entry is restored.
- **Entries deleted more than 30 days ago** aren't in the Archive any more. The agent can still find deletions up to 90 days old.
